## What it is

**Geoconquista** is a social geopolitical strategy game played in person, around a table. Each player builds up a power: they buy countries, complete regional blocs, recruit secret armies, invade, negotiate, spy and face unpredictable events.

It is a **hybrid** game. Conversations are face to face and the dice are thrown with a gesture: the player swipes on their phone and the dice land on the tablet, in full view of the table (D-08, D-13). A large screen shows the board, a tablet acts as the table console and each player has their power's private information on their phone. Technology calculates, keeps the secrets and provides the spectacle; **it does not replace the table**.

## Who it is for

- Groups of **3 to 6 people** (hypothesis to be validated) gathered at home, in games of 90 to 120 minutes.
- Families, with content suitable for minors (`familiar_u18`), and groups of adults with four social intensities: `familiar_18`, `party_18`, `party_extreme_18` and `parejas_18`.
- People who do not want to install anything: they join by scanning a QR.

## Pillars

1. **The table rules.** Negotiation happens out loud, the dice land on the tablet in the middle of the table, and the phone is only looked at to check secrets, confirm actions and throw the dice.
2. **Military secrecy is real.** Nobody knows a rival's exact troops: not the screen, not the console, not the rankings, not the logs, and not what can be deduced from spending. Only espionage reveals figures, and only to the one who spies.
3. **Diplomacy weighs as much as war.** There are formal treaties that the engine enforces, betrayal with consequences, and several ways to win.
4. **Chance is bounded.** The dice create excitement without deciding on their own: combat with auditable probabilities, trade to unblock blocs, and rules for whoever falls behind.
5. **It works without Internet.** Everything runs on the host's local network: no accounts, no cloud and no hidden dependencies.
6. **Content that is safe for each mode.** A truth or a dare can always be skipped. No dare requires harm, mandatory alcohol or non-consensual contact.
7. **Production quality** (D-04). Art, sound and animation on par with a commercial game, designed to be read from 2 m away and to perform on a Raspberry Pi and on modest phones.

## Surfaces

| Surface | Device | What it does | What it never does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public board (`/tablero`) | Large screen connected by HDMI to the host (Raspberry Pi 5 or laptop) | Track, map of real countries (D-06), owners, threat bands, events, spectacle and sound | Show exact troops, private balances or private cards |
| Table console (`/consola`) | Tablet: Android app as the main platform (D-02, D-03) or browser | Show the dice landing, resolve spaces, show public cards, pause | See secrets or run arbitrary commands such as “give money” |
| Private phone (`/jugador`) | Each player's phone browser, from a QR (D-02) | Money, countries, own troops, cards, treaties and public charts; confirm actions; throw the dice | Receive rivals' private data or decide the dice result: the host decides it |

## Creator requirements

These are R-01 to R-14 in the startup plan §4.1. The creator modified R-03 with D-08. The most visible ones:

- The name is Geoconquista. The screenshots say “Dominio Global”, and that name is not used.
- Virtual dice: the player swipes on their phone and the dice land on the tablet (D-08 and D-13, replacing the brief's real dice). The result is generated by the host.
- Map of real, present-day countries (D-06).
- Private treasury (D-07).
- Countries grouped in blocs of three; completing a bloc allows forming armies.
- Armies hidden from rivals.
- Tax Office, Immigration, Mail (email), Messaging and Truth or Dare spaces. “WhatsApp” remains only as an internal thematic reference, without its name or logo on screen (P-05).
- Five modes with the same core.
- Native Spanish, with all texts also in English; English voices and images with text are contributed by the community (D-28, D-30, `localization.md`).
- Open source and *Documentation First*.

## Experience goals

These are criteria CE-01 to CE-09 in the startup plan §2.2. The three that define the product's success:

- **CE-01:** a game lasts between 90 and 120 minutes with 3 or 4 players.
- **CE-03:** less than 30% of players feel they looked at their phone more than they wanted to.
- **CE-04:** zero unauthorized exposures of rival troops.

## Out of scope for the MVP

Online play and accounts; real WhatsApp; store apps for players; multiple maps or unit classes; real time; playable AI rival; extensive physical kit; languages other than Spanish and English; and any generative service during play, because it would break the no-Internet mode. The details are in the startup plan §3.2.
